Ask about academic pricing before annual billing

Apify discounts annual paid plans 10% and has separately mentioned institutional discounts. Get a quote before locking in a year at any tier.

10% off Starter is only ~$2/mo — worth asking, not worth chasing.
  • Prepaid credit expires. Don't upgrade a tier just to "bank" unused capacity — unused balance does not roll over to the next billing cycle.
  • Rented Store Actors can bill differently. Some charge per event rather than per CU; check each Actor's own pricing page before folding it into this fleet's budget.
  • A single recorded run is one data point. Treat the projector's CU/proxy defaults as a starting estimate, not a guarantee — a real production run against a bigger catalog or a retried WAF challenge can cost more.
  • Plan limits aren't the whole governance story. Credential storage, workspace ownership, access review, and data retention sit outside this pricing page entirely and are worth a separate pass with university IT.
